Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Building a playbook, the field guide, Ad math and the Wins board are free. You can even try it without an account; sign up to save your plans.

Do I need ChatGPT?

You need any AI chat: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot all work, and their free versions are fine. Marguru writes the prompts; you paste them in.

Can it replace an agency?

For many small businesses, much of the monthly work, yes: it's the same jobs, and you know your business best. The field guide explains when hiring still makes sense.

Where do the facts come from?

Google's and Meta's help pages, the FTC, the Ahrefs pricing survey and practitioners' write-ups. Every source is listed in the field guide, with how much to trust it.

Who sees my business details?

Only you. Saved plans live in your account; a plan made before signing up stays in your browser. Wins you post are public, under your display name only.

Will it get me results?

Nobody can promise that, and Marguru won't. It gives you the same steps a good agency follows; results depend on your business, your market and doing the work.
